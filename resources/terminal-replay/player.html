Recording details

Raw, unredacted output. Review before sharing. Includes visible drafts and cleared text. The conversation transcript is redacted separately.

Linked steps include prompt and response text, tool arguments and results. Their captured text is bounded and unredacted. Saved session details and request diagnostics are added from the separately redacted transcript when an exact match exists.

Long recordings continue in numbered parts. Only retained parts are included; older parts may have expired or been replaced.

A step links to the next completed terminal frame. Several steps can share a frame, and collapsed or offscreen content may not be visible.

Fonts, images and terminal-specific effects can differ from the original screen.
